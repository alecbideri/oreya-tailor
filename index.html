About Us

A tailoring house built on craft and care.

Oreya Tailor is a full-service tailoring and fashion house. From custom tailoring and fashion design to uniforms, bridal wear, traditional and contemporary clothing, alterations, accessories and textile production, we handle every stitch in-house.

Founded by Divine SINIGIRIRA and Aiba UMUHOZA, we also export garments and textile products — bringing Rwandan craftsmanship to clients beyond our borders.
